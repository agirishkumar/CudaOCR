# Benchmark results — custom kernels vs NPP (_Ctx)

Generated by `./build/bench` (20-iteration average, 2500×3500 8u image).
Hardware: RTX 3060 Laptop (sm_86, 6 GB), CUDA 13.1. Date: 2026-06-11.

| kernel | custom ms | NPP ms | ratio (custom/NPP) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| grayscale | 0.137 | 0.113 | 1.22× | within 2× target |
| gaussian 5×5 | 0.792 | 0.288 | 2.75× | follow-up task M1.10 |
| median 5×5 | 19.595 | 0.715 | 27.4× | follow-up task M1.9 |
| box mean 31×31 | 2.450 | 19.042 | 0.13× | **beats NPP 7.8×** (separable vs direct) |

Notes
- Median: per-thread 25-element insertion sort with global loads; NPP uses a
  histogram/sorting-network approach. Plan: shared-memory tiles + Paeth/network
  sort for 3×3, separable approximation or wavefront histogram for 5×5.
- Gaussian: global-memory taps; plan: shared-memory tile per block.
- The pipeline currently uses median 3×3 on ≤A4 scans (~2 ms) — acceptable
  until M1.9 lands.
